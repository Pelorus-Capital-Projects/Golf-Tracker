# Golf Tracker

Range sessions and rounds in one app: ball flight per club, hole-by-hole scoring with par 3/4/5, putts, fairways, greens in regulation, distances, scorecard photos or scans, and photos and videos from each round.

Tick the countries you've played under **Golfer → Countries played** and they light up in green on the world map behind the app. Map outlines: [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) (public domain).

**Open the app:** https://pelorus-capital-projects.github.io/Golf-Tracker/

## Put it on your iPhone Home Screen
1. Open the link above in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Open it from the **Golf** icon from then on. The Home Screen app keeps its own data, so log rounds there, not in a Safari tab.

## Keep your data safe
Everything is stored on the phone only. Use **Settings → Backup** regularly and save the file to Files or Dropbox. **Restore** brings it back on a new phone. Backups include scorecards and round photos; round videos are not included (too big), so keep the originals in your Photos app.

Single-file web app (`index.html`), no server, no tracking.
